ASK has moved into Nikash

The scorecard is now part of the console, so an interview is attached to the candidate rather than to a file on your laptop.

  1. Open Nikash and sign in
  2. Go to Candidates and open the person
  3. Under ASK interview, choose R1 screen or R2 interview

Same 42 questions, same 0–3 anchors. It now autosaves as you go, resumes if a call drops, records who ran it, and shows the result next to the questionnaire scores — including where the two disagree.
