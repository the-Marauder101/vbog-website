Three rules, enforced in the database

  1. Scores never leave the building. A client gets a person and a written recommendation. Never a dimension score, a match percentage, or the test.
  2. The candidate is assessed knowing nothing about any client. Everyone takes the identical 44 items, which is what makes one assessment usable against every open role.
  3. The system ranks and explains. It never decides. There is no reject button, and no column in the database that could hold one.
