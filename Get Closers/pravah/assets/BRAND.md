# Pravah Brand Assets

The Pravah mark represents **flow + upward movement**: the P is built as a
continuous ribbon and the rising arrow represents movement from people and
process into measurable performance.

## Files

- `pravah-mark.svg` — transparent symbol for navigation, favicon-style use and
  compact surfaces.
- `pravah-logo.svg` — full lockup with Pravah wordmark and `BY GET CLOSERS`.

## Visual language

- Primary gradient: indigo → blue → cyan.
- Wordmark: near-black on light backgrounds; use the mark alone on dark UI.
- Typography: Inter for product UI, DM Mono for operational labels/data.
- Keep generous clear space around the mark. Do not stretch, rotate or recolor
  the gradient arbitrarily.

## Brand position

Pravah is the Get Closers operating system from placement to performance and
revenue. It sits between the human/recruitment flow and measurable operating
outcomes without replacing Vyom or Nikash.
